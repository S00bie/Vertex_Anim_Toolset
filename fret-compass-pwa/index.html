WESTERN MODE (built on the root above)

All ten Hindustani thaats vs. the seven Western modes

Hindustani theory (Bhatkhande's system) recognizes ten parent scales, or thaats. Six share their exact interval pattern with a Western mode. Four — Bhairav, Marwa, Purvi, Todi — use combinations of naturals, flats and a raised 4th that no Western mode reproduces.
